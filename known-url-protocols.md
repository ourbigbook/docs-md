# Known URL protocols

↑ **Parent:** [OurBigBook Markup concepts](ourbigbook-markup-concepts.md)

Certain common URL protocols are treated as "known" by OurBigBook, and when found they have special effects in some parts of the conversion.

The currently known protocols are:
- `http://`
- `https://`

Effects of known protocols include:
- [shorthand link parsing rules](shorthand-link-parsing-rules.md): mark the start of shorthand links
- [store images in a separate media repository](store-images-in-a-separate-media-repository.md): mark an image `src` to ignore `provider`
