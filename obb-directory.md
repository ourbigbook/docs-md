<h1 id="obb-directory"><code>-/obb</code> directory</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Generated files](generated-files.md)

When publishing with [OurBigBook CLI](ourbigbook-cli.md), certain files such as the [`dist` directory](dist-directory.md) are placed under the [`-/obb` directory](obb-directory.md) on the final output.

Because `-/obb` is a [reserved ID](reserved-id.md), we can safely dump any autogenerated files under `-/obb` without fear of name conflicts with other files.
