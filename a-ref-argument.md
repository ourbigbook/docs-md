↑ **Parent:** [`\a` argument](a-argument.md)

Analogous to the [`\x` `ref` argument](x-ref-argument.md), e.g.:
```
Trump said this and that.https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_Access_Hollywood_tape#Trump's_responses{ref}https://web.archive.org/web/20161007210105/https://www.donaldjtrump.com/press-releases/statement-from-donald-j.-trump{ref} Then he said that and this.https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_Access_Hollywood_tape#Trump's_responses{ref}https://web.archive.org/web/20161007210105/https://www.donaldjtrump.com/press-releases/statement-from-donald-j.-trump{ref}
```

> Trump said this and that.[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_Access_Hollywood_tape#Trump's_responses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_Access_Hollywood_tape#Trump's_responses)[https://web.archive.org/web/20161007210105/https://www.donaldjtrump.com/press-releases/statement-from-donald-j.-trump](https://web.archive.org/web/20161007210105/https://www.donaldjtrump.com/press-releases/statement-from-donald-j.-trump) Then he said that and this.[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_Access_Hollywood_tape#Trump's_responses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump_Access_Hollywood_tape#Trump's_responses)[https://web.archive.org/web/20161007210105/https://www.donaldjtrump.com/press-releases/statement-from-donald-j.-trump](https://web.archive.org/web/20161007210105/https://www.donaldjtrump.com/press-releases/statement-from-donald-j.-trump)
