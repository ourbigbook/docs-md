# Donate

↑ **Parent:** [OurBigBook Project](split.md)

To donate:
- [https://cirosantilli.com/sponsor](https://cirosantilli.com/sponsor): give money directly to [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli.md)
- buy project merchandise, see: [Section "Merchandise"](merchandise.md)

All donated money currently just goes directly to [Ciro Santilli](ciro-santilli.md)'s personal bank account, the project founder and current lead. If things ever take off we will set up a legal entity to make things cleaner. One may dream. But for now, it would just add unnecessary overhead. Related: [Section "Project governance"](project-governance.md).

Ciro announces funding milestones and transparently accounts for all donations at: [https://cirosantilli.com/sponsor](https://cirosantilli.com/sponsor). When milestones are reached, he quits his day job and works full time on the project for a given amount of time.

We are also happy to discuss paid contracts to implement specific features, to get in touch see: [contact](contact.md).
