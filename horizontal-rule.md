# Horizontal rule

↑ **Parent:** [Macro](macro.md)  
🏷️ **Tags:** [Implemented by sidstuff](implemented-by-sidstuff.md)

Used to represent a thematic break between paragraph-level elements:
```
She pressed the button. Just like that, everything was over.

\Hr

The next morning was a gloomy one. Nobody said a word.
```
which renders as:



> She pressed the button. Just like that, everything was over.
> 
> ---
> 
> The next morning was a gloomy one. Nobody said a word.

This macro corresponds to a misfeature of HTML/Markdown, and is not encouraged. We instead recommend creating smaller more specific [headers](header.md) instead to split sections, as this has all the usual advantages of allowing metadata to be associated to the header, such as [`-S`, `--split-headers`](split-headers.md), [topic](ourbigbook-web-topics.md), liked and discussions.

But they people asked, and they got it.
