<h1 id="ourbigbook-web-unlisted-articles">OurBigBook Web unlisted content</h1>

↑ **Parent:** [Local header deletion on web upload](local-header-deletion-on-web-upload.md)

It is possible to mark articles, discussions and comments as unlisted in [OurBigBook Web](ourbigbook-web.md).

This also happens automatically when doing [`-W`, `--web`](web.md) upload for previously published articles that were deleted locally, see also: [Section "Local header deletion on web upload"](local-header-deletion-on-web-upload.md)

Marking an article as unlisted makes it not show up by default on article listings including:
- as descendants of an article when seen on the each article page, i.e. on the Table of contents and below due to [dynamic article tree](ourbigbook-web-dynamic-article-tree.md)
- on global or per-user listings of latest and top article
- on [topics](ourbigbook-web-topics.md)
Unlisted articles do however appear on listings such as:
- lists of articles liked by given users

Discussions and comments can likewise be unlisted by their authors or by an administrator. They are hidden from listings by default, but each affected listing offers controls to show all content or only unlisted content.
