# Disambiguate shows on navigation

↑ **Parent:** [News](../news-split.md)

<a id="_174"></a>
The [`\H` `disambiguate` argument](../disambiguate-argument.md) now shows on most navigation locations including:<a id="_175"></a>

<a id="_176"></a>
- article indices on [`-W`, `--web`](../web.md)
<a id="_177"></a>
- tagged/incoming article lists on [`-W`, `--web`](../web.md) and [static](../p-publish.md)
<a id="_178"></a>
- breadcrumb navigation on [`-W`, `--web`](../web.md) and [static](../p-publish.md)
This makes it easier to understand what the topic is about when [disambiguate](../disambiguate-argument.md) is fundamental.
