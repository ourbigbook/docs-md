<h1 id="web-force"><code>--web-force</code></h1>

↑ **Parent:** [OurBigBook CLI options](ourbigbook-cli-options.md)

Replace the current web build without confirmation. Implies [`-W`, `--web`](web.md). Previous job history and staged payloads are removed, but articles and committed conversion checkpoints are preserved. This does not force re-extraction or re-rendering; use [`--web-force-id-extraction`](web-force-id-extraction.md) or [`--web-force-render`](web-force-render.md) for that.
