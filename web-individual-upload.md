<h1 id="web-individual-upload"><code>--web-individual-upload</code></h1>

↑ **Parent:** [OurBigBook CLI options](ourbigbook-cli-options.md)

Upload to [OurBigBook Web](ourbigbook-web.md) one article at a time.

This is an older upload mechanism. This option was added mostly as a debug fallback in case there are issues with the newer [background build](background-build.md) mechanism.

It could however also be faster in case the background build queue is full and you are doing a single upload.
