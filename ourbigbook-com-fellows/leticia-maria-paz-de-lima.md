2022-12: Letícia Maria Paz De Lima is awarded 10,000 Brazilian Real (~1,929 USD) to help her:<a id="ourbigbook-com-fellows/_5716"></a>


> Focus on her [quantum computing](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/quantum-computing) studies and research until 2023-06-30 (end of her third year), with the future intention of pursuing a [PhD](https://ourbigbook.com/cirosantilli/doctor-of-philosophy) abroad in that area.

<a id="ourbigbook-com-fellows/_5717"></a>
At the time of award, Letícia was a 3rd year student at the [Molecular Sciences Course of the University of São Paulo](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/molecular-sciences-course-of-the-university-of-sao-paulo) and held a [FAPESP Scientific Initiation Scholarship](https://fapesp.br/bolsas/ic). She had become interested in Quantum Computing in the past year, and is passionate about working on that promising area of technology.

<a id="ourbigbook-com-fellows/_5718"></a>
Her main mentors in the area so far were [professor Paulo Nussenzveig](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FbGL0BEAAAAJ&hl=it) and [Barbara Amaral](https://portal.if.usp.br/ifusp/pt-br/users/bamaral) of the Institute of Physics of the [University of São Paulo](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/university-of-sao-paulo).
