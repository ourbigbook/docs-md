# OurBigBook Web user manual

↑ **Parent:** [OurBigBook Web](ourbigbook-web.md)

[OurBigBook Web](ourbigbook-web.md) is a bit like [Wikipedia](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/wikipedia), but where each user can have their own version of each page, and it [cannot be edited by others without permission](can-my-ourbigbook-com-content-be-edited-or-removed-by-other-users.md).

And it is also a bit like [Obsidian](https://ourbigbook.com/go/topic/obsidian) (a personal knowledge base): you can optionally keep all your notes in plaintext markup files in your computer and publish either on OurBigBook.com or as a static HTML website on your own domain.

The goal of the [OurBigBook Project](split.md) is to make university students write perfect natural sciences books for free as they are trying to learn for their lectures.

<a id="video-introduction-to-the-ourbigbook-project-ourbigbook-web-user-manual"></a>
**[Video 14](#video-introduction-to-the-ourbigbook-project-ourbigbook-web-user-manual). Introduction to the OurBigBook Project.** [Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JOJYx0mmhg).

Suppose that Mr. Barack Obama is your calculus teacher this semester.

Being an enlightened teacher, Mr. Obama writes everything that he knows on his OurBigBook.com account. His home page look something like the following tree:
- [https://ourbigbook.com/barack-obama](https://ourbigbook.com/barack-obama): Obama's toplevel index pages linking to all his pages
  - [Mathematics](https://ourbigbook.com/barack-obama/mathematics)
    - [Calculus](https://ourbigbook.com/barack-obama/calculus)
      - [Derivative](https://ourbigbook.com/barack-obama/derivative)
      - [Integral](https://ourbigbook.com/barack-obama/integral)
        - [Fundamental theorem of calculus](https://ourbigbook.com/barack-obama/fundamental-theorem-of-calculus)
    - [Algebra](https://ourbigbook.com/barack-obama/algebra)
  - [Physics](https://ourbigbook.com/barack-obama/physics)

On your first day of class, Mr. Obama tells his students to read the "Calculus" section, ask him any questions that come up online, and just walks away. No time wasted!

While you are working through the sections under "Calculus", you happen to notice that the "Fundamental theorem of calculus" article is a bit hard to understand. Mr. Obama is a good teacher, but no one can write perfect tutorials of every little thing, right?

This is where OurBigBook comes to your rescue. There are two ways that it can help you solve the problem:
- [OurBigBook Web topics](ourbigbook-web-topics.md)
- [OurBigBook Web discussions](ourbigbook-web-discussions.md)
